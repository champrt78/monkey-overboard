## Discoveries / Gotchas
- The scipy border-flood needs a near-white threshold (`max > 245`) rather than the literal `> 200` from the brief; `> 200` ate the bright yellow banana and water-foam highlights.
- The first gator regeneration came out at 240×48 (48×48 frames). This would have broken `gator_frames.tres`, which expects 64×24 frames on a 320×24 sheet. Re-ran with the corrected prompt/dimensions.
- The old `generate_sprites.py` prompts were changed by the generating agent to ask for a solid white background. The border-flood handles white, gray, or checkerboard mats, so this is fine, but final dimensions must stay locked to the SpriteFrames atlases.
